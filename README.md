## 📌 Project Overview

LOGIFORGE AI is a software-based Predictive Logistics & Forward Supply Chain Decision Support System designed to connect multiple logistics planning activities into a single operational planning workflow.

The platform helps logistics planners:

- Monitor inventory across locations
- Analyse consumption patterns
- Generate demand forecasts
- Detect potential stock-out risks
- Manage supply requests
- Monitor transport assets and capacity
- Compare synthetic planning routes
- Generate allocation recommendations
- Simulate logistics disruption scenarios
- Understand why a recommendation was generated
- Keep human decision-makers in the loop

The system follows a continuous decision-support cycle:

```text
OBSERVE
   ↓
PREDICT
   ↓
DETECT
   ↓
OPTIMIZE
   ↓
RECOMMEND
   ↓
MONITOR
